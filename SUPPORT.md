## Filing a Bug Report

Before filing, please:

1. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/grith-ai/grith/issues) to avoid duplicates
2. Check the [troubleshooting guide](https://docs.grith.ai/docs/guides/troubleshooting)
3. Include in your report:
   - `grith --version` output
   - Operating system and version
   - Steps to reproduce
   - Expected vs actual behaviour
   - Relevant log output (`RUST_LOG=grith_supervisor=debug grith exec ...`)
